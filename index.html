Level 1

Night Yard

You're a mechanical ball, switched on in a dark scrapyard. You can roll, but there's no jump module fitted yet.

  • Tilt your phone left or right to roll. Speed carries through every drop.
  • Drops of about 4 m or more will break you. Your light turns red when a fall is too high.
  • On a lift, press Use to ride it.
  • Breaking sends you back to the last green checkpoint.

This page isn't on an https:// address, so the gyro is blocked. You'll get ◀ ▶ buttons instead.
